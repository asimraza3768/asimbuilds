The Value of Building Things From Scratch

October 22, 2026 | 3 min read

Tutorials are safe. They provide a clear path, curated data, and guaranteed results. But the real world of software and data engineering is rarely curated.

The Illusion of Competence

It is easy to follow along with a video course and feel like you have mastered PySpark or AWS. However, the moment you open an empty code editor with a real-world objective and zero instructions, that illusion shatters. You realize you don't actually know how to authenticate the API, or how to handle the specific JSON pagination the documentation forgot to mention.

Embracing the Blank Canvas

My biggest leaps in understanding happened when I forced myself to build end-to-end projects—like my Job Market Intelligence Platform—completely from scratch. Yes, it took ten times longer. Yes, I spent days debugging simple configuration errors. But the friction of figuring it out on my own built muscle memory that no tutorial could ever provide.

If you want to accelerate your career, close the guide. Pick a problem. Open a blank file. Start typing.
