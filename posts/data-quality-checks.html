Why Data Quality Checks Matter Before Analytics

October 5, 2026 | 4 min read

Garbage in, garbage out. If you feed unvalidated API data directly into a BI dashboard, your analytics will be fundamentally wrong. In my Job Market pipeline, data must pass through six validation categories in the Silver layer before it is allowed to reach Gold.

The 6 Validation Categories

  • Required Fields: Dropping rows that lack critical IDs or job titles.
  • Duplicates: Removing identical job postings scraped multiple times.
  • Salaries: Filtering out malformed or unrealistic salary strings.
  • Timestamps: Casting strings to proper Date/Timestamp types.
  • Remote Values: Standardizing booleans (e.g., converting "Yes", "Y", "True" to a standard format).
  • Country Codes: Ensuring the 5 targeted cities map correctly to Pakistan.

Enforcing these rules using PySpark ensures that when a query runs against the Gold layer, the engineer doesn't have to write messy `WHERE` clauses to avoid bad data.
