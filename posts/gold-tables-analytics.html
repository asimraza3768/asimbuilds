Designing Analytics-Ready Data With Gold Tables

September 18, 2026 | 4 min read

The Silver layer contains a clean, validated history of job postings. But if you connect a BI tool directly to Silver and ask it to calculate "The most in-demand skills by city over the last 30 days," the compute engine has to scan thousands of rows on the fly.

Building the 6 Gold Tables

To optimize the Databricks AI/BI dashboard, I created 6 specific Gold tables. These tables pre-aggregate the data. For example, instead of storing individual job rows, a skill_demand_summary table simply holds the skill name, the city, and the total count.

By doing the heavy computational lifting inside the Databricks Workflow overnight, the dashboard itself becomes lightning fast for the end-user, loading pre-calculated metrics instantly.
