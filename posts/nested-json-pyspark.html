What I Learned From Working With Nested JSON

September 22, 2026 | 4 min read

APIs rarely return data in a clean tabular format. When fetching job listings, the JSON payload often includes nested structures—for example, a `skills` array inside a `job_details` struct.

Exploding Arrays in PySpark

Relational databases and analytics tables require flat rows. In my Silver layer, I had to use PySpark's explode() function to turn arrays of skills into individual rows so they could be properly aggregated in the Gold layer.

This introduced a new challenge: exploding an array multiplies the number of rows. Suddenly, one job posting with five skills becomes five rows. Managing unique identifiers and ensuring joins remained accurate taught me the importance of careful schema design before jumping into PySpark transformations.
