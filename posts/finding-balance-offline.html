Finding Balance in a Screen-Heavy World

October 25, 2026 | 3 min read

As a Computer Science student and an aspiring Data Engineer, my life revolves around screens. Between writing code, reading documentation, and debugging pipelines, it is incredibly easy to spend twelve hours a day staring at glowing rectangles.

The Need to Disconnect

I realized recently that the quality of my work decreases when I don't give my mind time to rest. Burnout doesn't always look like exhaustion; sometimes it looks like a complete lack of creativity or patience for problem-solving.

Analog Anchors

This is why having interests completely disconnected from technology is so vital. Writing poetry, reading a physical book, or simply stepping outside shifts the brain into a different mode of thinking. It grounds you. Ironically, taking a step away from the keyboard is often the fastest way to solve a complex coding problem. The mind just needs space to breathe.
