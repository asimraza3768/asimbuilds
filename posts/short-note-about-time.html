A Short Note About Time

September 20, 2026 · 1 min read · By Asim Raza
We measure distance in miles and milestones, but progress is often measured in patience. There is a quiet, difficult space between starting and arriving, where the road simply stretches forward without offering any promises. I used to rush through this space. I wanted the certainty of the destination more than I wanted to understand the journey. Now, I am learning to trust the quiet hours. To continue walking even when the map feels blank. Because time does not demand that we have all the answers, it only asks that we keep moving.
